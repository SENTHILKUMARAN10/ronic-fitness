A simple explanation of how this website handles your visit requests.

How the visit form works

When you fill out the visit request form, the website prepares a WhatsApp message containing the details you entered and opens WhatsApp. Your information is not sent to the gym unless you choose to send that message in WhatsApp. This version of the website does not operate a database or store form submissions.

What information you choose to share

The form requests your name, phone number, training interest, optional preferred date/time and your consent to share these details via WhatsApp. The gym can use the information you send to respond to your enquiry and discuss an appointment or membership. Please contact the gym for information about its own recordkeeping practices.

Third-party services

The site links to WhatsApp, Instagram and Google Maps. These services have their own privacy practices. Google Fonts may be requested to render the site's typography. No analytics or advertising tracking has been added to this initial website.

Booking and payments

Submitting the form does not reserve a time slot. The gym must confirm availability directly. This website does not accept or process online payments.
